Tim

Owner/Chef

Tim has been a lifelong foodie with a deep-rooted passion for exceptional flavors and top-notch service. With years of experience in both fine dining and casual dining, he's honed his craft as a culinary pro, working in kitchens where quality and creativity reign supreme.

After traveling far and wide, tasting burgers from coast to coast, Tim noticed a gap in the food scene—there simply wasn't a slider that lived up to his gourmet standards. So, he decided to change the game.

"We chose to focus on sliders because we want to put a high-end meal in the palm of your hand. Everyone loves burgers, so we decided to put our gourmet touch and creativity on the classic slider."
